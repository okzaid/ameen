Every currency, honestly

No more guessing what your dirhams are worth in dollars.

Most apps squash everything into one currency with an exchange rate you never chose. Ameen shows each currency on its own line. Flip the switch above if you'd rather see one converted total.

⃃

The new UAE Dirham sign is built in, so AED amounts look the way they should.

Account groups

Banks, cash, cards. Grouped, with their own totals.

Put accounts into groups you name yourself. Each group shows what it holds in every currency, and dragging an account moves it between groups.
